## 2025-04-08 - Accessible Tooltips for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** In the `Records.tsx` page, the "Filter" and "More Actions" buttons were icon-only without proper ARIA labels or visual hints. Wrapping them in `@/components/ui/tooltip` components alongside adding explicit `aria-label`s significantly improved accessibility for screen readers and provided much-needed context to sighted users upon hover.
**Action:** When creating or maintaining icon-only buttons across the app, always utilize the `Tooltip` component (via `TooltipProvider`, `TooltipTrigger`, `TooltipContent`) and include an `aria-label` to ensure a consistent, accessible experience.

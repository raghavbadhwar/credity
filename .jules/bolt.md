## 2025-02-13 - Promise.all vs Sequential Await
**Learning:** O(N) operations in `for...of` loops are a major performance bottleneck for bulk operations.
**Action:** Always consider using `Promise.all` with `Array.map` for concurrent execution of asynchronous operations where order is not strictly required.

## 2024-03-03 - [Mobile Dashboard Performance]
**Learning:** Found manual data fetching with `useState` and `useEffect` in `apps/mobile/src/screens/holder-dashboard-screen.tsx`, `issuer-dashboard-screen.tsx`, and `recruiter-dashboard-screen.tsx`. The project already has `@tanstack/react-query` installed and configured in `App.tsx` but it is not being utilized in the dashboard components. Fetching 7-8 different endpoints in parallel without caching or deduplication via `Promise.all` means full refetch on every render when returning to the screen, significantly degrading performance, increasing bandwidth, and delaying Time to Interactive.
**Action:** Migrate manual `useEffect`/`Promise.all` data fetching to React Query's `useQuery`/`useQueries` hooks to benefit from caching, stale-while-revalidate, and deduplication out of the box. Focus on migrating one of the heavy dashboards (like `holder-dashboard-screen`) first.
